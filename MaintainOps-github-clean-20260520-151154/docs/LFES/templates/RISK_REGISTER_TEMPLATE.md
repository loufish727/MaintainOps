## Risk Fields

- Risk: concise description.
- Severity: Critical, High, Medium, Low, Informational.
- Area: Security, Database, Reliability, Maintainability, Scalability, Reviewability, Deployment, Mobile.
- Evidence: file, function, SQL, QA log entry, or observation.
- Mitigation: planned correction.
- Verification: how the fix will be proven.
