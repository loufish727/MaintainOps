# LFES Severity Levels

## Critical

Could expose cross-company data, bypass authentication, corrupt live operational records, permanently lose important traceability, or prevent core app access. Requires immediate attention before production/release.

## High

Could break important workflows, weaken authorization, hide operational state, cause wrong-location work, or make future changes unsafe. Should be fixed before major release or onboarding real companies.

## Medium

Creates maintainability, reliability, or reviewability risk that is manageable now but likely to grow. Should be scheduled soon.

## Low

Useful improvement with limited immediate risk. Can be grouped with related work.

## Informational

Context, passed area, assumption, or observation that helps future reviewers but does not require immediate action.
